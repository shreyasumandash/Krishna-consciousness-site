Holy Places of Devotion

India is blessed with many sacred places connected with the pastimes of Lord Krishna and His devotees. These holy places inspire millions of people to develop love and devotion for Krishna.

Vrindavan

The sacred land where Lord Krishna performed many of His childhood pastimes.

Mayapur

The birthplace of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu and an important center of devotion.

Jagannath Puri

One of India's most famous holy places, known for Lord Jagannath and Ratha Yatra.

Dwarka

An ancient sacred city closely connected with Lord Krishna.

ISKCON Temple

ISKCON temples around the world help spread Krishna consciousness.

Govardhan

A sacred place remembered for Lord Krishna lifting Govardhan Hill.
