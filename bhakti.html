A Life of Devotion

Bhakti is the path of loving devotional service to Lord Krishna. Simple daily practices can help us remember Krishna and develop a deeper spiritual connection.

Chanting

Chanting the "Hare Krishna maha-mantra" helps us remember Krishna and purify the heart.

Reading

Reading scriptures such as the Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagwatam helps us understand spiritual knowledge.

Prasadam

Preparing and honoring food offered to Krishna is a loving act of devotion.

Association

Associating with devotees encourages us on the spiritual path and helps us grow in bhakti.
